# Bento Stats Privacy Policy

Bento Stats does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your Mac

Bento Stats reads your Mac's statistics (CPU, GPU, memory, network, storage, battery,
temperatures, fans and running processes) from macOS itself, and shows them to you. They
are never sent anywhere. The app keeps its settings and up to a day of history on your
Mac, in its own folder, and nowhere else.

## Network requests

**Your public IP address.** If you turn on **Look up my public IP address** in Settings
(it is off until you do), Bento Stats asks [ipify](https://www.ipify.org) for your public
IP address, so it can show it to you. Like any web request, it reaches ipify from your IP
address; Bento Stats sends nothing else with it.

**Update checks, in the version downloaded from GitHub only.** That version asks GitHub
once a day whether a newer release exists, and downloads it from GitHub when you choose to
install it. The request reaches GitHub from your IP address and carries nothing about you.
Turn off **Check for updates automatically** in Settings to stop the daily check. The Mac
App Store version is updated by the App Store and makes no such request.

With both off, Bento Stats makes no network requests at all.
